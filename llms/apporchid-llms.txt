# App Orchid

> App Orchid is an enterprise AI platform that makes business data AI-ready through a patented semantic knowledge graph. Its semantic (or "context") layer sits between fragmented enterprise data and AI agents, BI tools, and applications—capturing relationships, business logic, and meaning so LLMs can reason over data accurately instead of hallucinating. The flagship product, Easy Answers™, lets users ask questions in plain English and get explainable, traceable analytics, insights, and recommendations.

App Orchid's core thesis is that accuracy is the bottleneck for enterprise AI, and that a semantic layer closes the "context gap" between what AI models see in raw data and what the business actually means. The platform automates ontology discovery and enrichment with LLMs, connects to data where it lives (lakehouses, SQL databases, SaaS apps, and unstructured documents) without forcing a single warehouse, and is model-agnostic—supporting OpenAI, Google Gemini, Anthropic Claude, Meta Llama, Mistral, DeepSeek, and Azure OpenAI. It is agent-ready, with support for Model Context Protocol (MCP), Google Agentspace, and ontology-driven Text2SQL.
